Who We Are

From a Small Office to a Trusted Name

MI Real Estate & Construction started with a clear goal — to make property buying, selling and construction in Karachi simple, safe and transparent. Over the years, we have helped hundreds of families find verified plots and homes in Naya Nazimabad and Bahria Town Karachi, and built quality homes with our in-house construction team.

Today, we have taken the next step in our journey: developing our own community, Al Jannat City — a project where our real estate insight and construction expertise come together.
